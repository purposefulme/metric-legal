Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

iOS: Open the Settings app on your iPhone > tap your Apple ID profile at the top > Subscriptions > select Agentic Champion to manage or cancel.
Android: Open the Google Play Store app > tap your profile icon at top right > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > select Agentic Champion.

How does the 3-day free trial work?

Eligible new subscribers receive full access to all companion features, study windows, and shielding tools for 3 days free of charge. You can cancel at any time in your device's subscription settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

How do I restore my existing subscription on a new device?

Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google Play account that originally purchased the subscription. On the paywall screen inside the app, tap "Restore Purchases" to instantly reactivate your entitlement.

Why is app shielding or screen time tracking not appearing?

App shielding and distraction analytics require system permissions to function. On iOS, enable Screen Time permissions when prompted. On Android, grant "Usage Access" in your device Settings under Special App Access.

How can I request account or data deletion?

You can request full deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time by emailing us at purposefuldrivenllc@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
