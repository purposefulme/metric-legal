Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Metric LLC ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how Agentic Champion ("the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Our Core Privacy Principle: Your screen time, habit history, and personal schedule belong to you. We design our focus and shielding systems to process sensitive activity on-device whenever possible.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address, user ID, and basic authentication details (processed securely via Firebase Authentication).
  • Planning & Habits Data: Tasks, courses, study windows, milestones, custom routine activities, and daily schedules you create within the App.
  • Preferences & Settings: Your theme, notification preferences, and time budget configurations (sleep, commute, lecture hours).

B. Device & Screen Time Permissions (iOS Family Controls)

To provide app shielding, focus block sessions, and screen habit awareness:

  • Screen Time & Family Controls (iOS): With your explicit consent, the App interacts with Apple's FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity frameworks.
  • On-Device Processing: Specific apps and categories you select to shield or restrict are processed locally on your device via Apple’s privacy-preserving APIs. We do not sell, track, or transmit your individual browsing history or personal app usage details to external servers.

C. Purchases & Subscriptions Data

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store and managed via RevenueCat. We receive anonymous subscription status (such as active entitlement tier, purchase dates, and expiration) to grant you access to premium features. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers or banking information.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Information

We may collect non-identifiable technical data (e.g., device model, OS version, app crashes) strictly to maintain application stability, security, and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and synchronize your tasks, study windows, routines, and habits across your sessions.
  • Enforce custom distraction limits and app shielding during active focus sessions.
  • Verify and manage your active subscription status.
  • Send optional reminders and schedule alerts you have configured.
  • Improve app performance, diagnose technical errors, and enhance user experience.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase: For secure authentication and encrypted cloud database synchronization.
  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app subscription entitlements and receipt verification.
  • Apple Inc.: For in-app purchase processing, push notifications, and native Screen Time / Family Controls frameworks.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as your account is active or needed to provide services. You can delete your account and associated cloud data at any time from within the App settings or by contacting our support team at purposefuldrivenllc@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
